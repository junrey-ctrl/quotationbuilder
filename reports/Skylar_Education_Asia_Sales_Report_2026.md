## Definitions and data limitations

- **Awarded:** only SE-2026-005.1 and SE-2026-006.2, based on management's explicit confirmation.
- **Proposal:** a quotation submitted but not confirmed as awarded.
- **Superseded:** an earlier quotation version replaced by a later revision; do not add it to the live pipeline.
- **Awarded contract value:** quoted value of awarded scope; not proof of payment, collection, revenue recognition or training completion.
- **Currency:** USD. No PHP conversion is included.
- **Not provided:** payment status, invoice number, actual collection, training date, completion status, direct cost and gross margin. Add these fields when supporting records are available.
