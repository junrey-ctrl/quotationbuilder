Management interpretation & next actions

  • 1. Build a live follow-up pipeline: assign an accountable owner, next action, due date, decision-maker and probability to every pending proposal; prioritize the four priced proposals by value and expiry.
  • 2. Close the pricing gaps: obtain customer-selected scope, participant count and approved total for the five unpriced schedules before counting them as monetary pipeline.
  • 3. Improve conversion visibility: capture submitted date, follow-up history, won/lost/on-hold outcome, loss reason and decision date; calculate conversion only from a complete decided cohort.
  • 4. Separate sales from cash: add PO/contract reference, invoice number/date, due date, amount billed, amount collected and outstanding balance; reconcile monthly with Finance.
  • 5. Measure delivery economics: record confirmed training dates, participant attendance/completion, trainer and venue costs, travel costs, gross margin and repeat-business potential.
Conversion, collection, recognised revenue, completion and margin are not calculated where supporting source data is missing.

Official channels

What to improve

  • Google reviews: request feedback after each completed training, reply promptly, and monitor rating and review volume monthly.
  • Content consistency: publish a repeatable mix of training outcomes, safety tips, instructor expertise, course dates and customer stories.
  • Lead conversion: use a clear enquiry CTA and track website clicks, form submissions, calls and qualified leads by platform.
  • Creative testing: compare short video, carousel and case-study posts using reach, saves, shares and enquiries—not likes alone.
  • Measurement: record monthly baselines and campaign links with UTM tags; do not add platform audiences together as unique people.
